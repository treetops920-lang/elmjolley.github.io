About Me

Student • IT • Future Computer Engineer

Hello, I'm Elm Jolley. I am Graduate of Kettering Fairmont High School as of May 2026, and will be attending the University of Toledo in fall 2026. I'll be pursuing a degree in Computer Science and Engineering, at UToledo. This website is a way for me to share my journey, experiences, and background with you.

My Background

In my Information Technology program, I have learned programming languages including HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I have also gained experience with hardware, networking, and cybersecurity. Through hands-on projects and real-world scenarios, I have developed stronger problem-solving skills and learned how to work collaboratively in a team environment.

My time in the program has strengthened my interest in technology and helped confirm that I want to pursue Computer Engineering. It has also given me the opportunity to intern at The Education Connection, where I have been able to gain practical experience and build confidence in my technical skills.

Outside of technology, I have played viola in my school orchestra for the past six years. I have also worked consistently since sophomore year, beginning with Huey Magoo’s and continuing through several jobs. Those experiences taught me work ethic, teamwork, adaptability, and how to function in fast-paced environments without falling apart dramatically.

Growing up in a military family meant I moved often, including living in different parts of the United States and Germany. Because of that, I learned how to adapt quickly, meet new people, and make the best of unfamiliar situations. That flexibility has shaped how I approach school, work, and future opportunities.
